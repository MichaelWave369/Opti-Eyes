# Opti-Eyes | Nine gates before physical claims

**Intent:** investigate truthful adaptive eyewear with a modular GEN-E companion. This is research planning, not medical or electrical engineering approval.

1. **Assumptions:** document focal model, measurement geometry, accommodation vs. refractive error, and all excluded populations/use cases.
2. **Optical bench:** compare candidate variable-focus mechanisms and IP constraints; measure resolution, distortion, power range, field of view, aberrations, temporal response, and failure positions on an optical bench.
3. **Mechanical safety:** use guarded moving parts, impact-resistant frames, breakaway/cable safety, and documented adjustment travel.
4. **Electrical safety:** assess battery, heat, charging, ingress, ESD/EMC and local control integrity under applicable standards.
5. **Sensor failure:** test near/far ambiguity, low reflectivity, occlusion, dark scenes, latency, power loss and manual override.
6. **Wearer comfort:** evaluate weight distribution, eye relief, fit, nausea, display brightness, and fatigue under appropriate professional oversight.
7. **Professional fitting:** obtain optometrist/ophthalmologist advice for prescription compatibility; this project never substitutes for an eye exam or prescribes corrections.
8. **Regulatory assessment:** determine applicable eyewear, UV protection, electronics, battery, accessibility, and potentially medical-device requirements for each intended market before shipping.
9. **Independent replication:** publish reproducible procedures and credible third-party optical measurements before advertising performance.

## Design separations

- Electronic variable focus is **not equivalent** to inexpensive fixed/multifocal lenses.
- Certified UV protection requires objective measurements; dark tint alone is not evidence.
- Evidence for broad blue-light eye-strain relief claims is limited; do not build marketing around them.
- AR overlays can impair situational awareness. Treat any future device as not approved for driving or other safety-critical activity unless a qualified regulatory and safety program establishes otherwise.
- Physical GEN.E.WRIST is a future hardware companion. Browser WorldAdapter demos are not device communications.

For now, the only deliverable is a synthetic, inspectable lab.
